The Practice

Every place has a story waiting to be discovered.

At Mehta Design Atelier (MDA), we believe architecture is not the act of creating buildings — it is the craft of shaping experiences. It is where people gather, where ideas evolve, where memories are made, and where everyday life quietly unfolds. Our work begins by listening: to the land, to the context, to the aspirations of those who will inhabit a space.

Founded through the shared vision of an architect and a structural engineer, MDA was built on the belief that exceptional design emerges when creativity and engineering evolve together. Architecture gives form to an idea; structure gives it strength — neither exists in isolation.

We are drawn to clarity over complexity, purpose over excess, and timelessness over trends. Every line drawn carries intention; every material chosen serves a purpose.

Vatsal Mehta

Srushti Sheth
Structural Engineer & Co-Founder

Srushti Sheth is a Structural Engineer and Co-Founder of Mehta Design Atelier (MDA), where she believes the strength of every building lies not only in its structure but in the harmony between engineering and design.

She brings professional experience across residential, commercial, and institutional developments, contributing across every stage of the structural design process — from concept development to on-site coordination.

At MDA, Srushti works collaboratively with architects to develop structural systems that are efficient, practical, and seamlessly integrated with the architectural vision.
